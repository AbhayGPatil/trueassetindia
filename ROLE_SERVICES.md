# TrueAssets Role Services Guide

## 1. 🏠 OWNER
**Service**: List properties and manage sales/rentals with full control over pricing, descriptions, media, and buyer interactions
- Post unlimited properties (residential, commercial, land)
- Upload photos and videos
- Set prices and listing terms
- Track inquiries and interested buyers
- Receive property verification within 48 hours
- Get verified badge on profile
- No listing fees

---

## 2. 🤝 BROKER
**Service**: Manage client properties, connect buyers with properties, and build professional broker profile for lead generation
- List multiple client properties in one dashboard
- Access pre-verified property database
- Build professional broker profile with verification badge
- Showcase completed transactions and client testimonials
- Receive buyer inquiries and property requests
- Commission-based business model
- Featured broker listings on homepage

---

## 3. 🏗️ DEVELOPER
**Service**: Launch real estate development projects with comprehensive project details, media gallery, and buyer lead management
- Create project profiles with detailed specifications:
  - Location and layout details
  - Unit types and configurations
  - Amenities and facilities
  - Price and payment plans
  - Project timeline and milestones
- Upload project media (images, floor plans, videos)
- Manage leads and buyer inquiries
  - Track interested buyers
  - Communicate directly with prospects
  - Export lead data
- Project analytics and visibility stats
- Featured projects on homepage
- Direct buyer connections without broker intermediary

---

## 4. ✍️ NOTARY
**Service**: Offer professional notarization and legal documentation services to property buyers with verified credentials
- Create professional notary profile with:
  - Registration details and verification
  - Years of experience and expertise areas
  - Profile picture and professional bio
  - Service locations and specializations
- Get published and visible to buyers (₹100 one-time)
- Verified professional badge
- Display credentials:
  - Registration number
  - Areas of expertise (property registration, stamp duty, etc.)
  - Number of documents processed
  - Years of experience
- Featured in notary directory on homepage
- Direct contact from property buyers
- Badge upgrades based on completed services (Silver/Gold/Platinum)

## Authentication & Role Assignment

- Users sign up selecting their role (OWNER, BROKER, DEVELOPER, NOTARY, BUYER, ADVOCATE)
- Each role is verified and gets corresponding dashboard access
- Role-specific permissions and features are automatically enabled
- Users can request role upgrades through settings

---

## Payment Models

- **OWNER**: Free (no fees)
- **BROKER**: Commission-based (on successful transactions)
- **DEVELOPER**: Project promotion packages available
- **NOTARY**: ₹100 one-time for profile publication
